## 3. THE CORE STORY (use this as the narrative spine for Hero + About)

Most of Aalok's CS classmates spent their degree chasing an internship at an IT company. Aalok went a different way: right after finishing high school in 2022, he started working inside his father's two-person travel agency — not as a favor, but because he saw a business still running entirely on Excel sheets, paper ledgers, and manual ticket tracking, and he wanted to see if he could actually fix it, not just study how to fix things.

Nepal, broadly, is still a country where a huge amount of basic office work — documentation, record-keeping, ticketing, approvals — happens on paper or in scattered spreadsheets, even while the rest of the world is moving toward AI-assisted, automated workflows. Aalok's instinct is that the most useful thing a young engineer in Nepal can do right now isn't to chase a job abroad — it's to start closing that gap, one real business at a time, starting with the one closest to him.

That's what this portfolio documents: a computer science student who used his own family's company as a live testbed for real software — accounting systems, cloud backup, digital records, B2B client management — while still carrying a full course load. It is not finished. It's the first chapter.

## 15. DESIGN SYSTEM

- **Mode:** Dark-first (matches the provided headshot's tone — near-black background, warm-neutral text, soft single-source lighting accents).
- **Palette:** Near-black base (#0A0A0B – #111113), off-white text (#F2F2F0), one restrained accent color — warm amber/gold (echoing the rim-light in the headshot, e.g. #D9A25C) or a cool signal blue (#5B8CFF) — pick one accent family only, use it sparingly for links/highlights/CTAs.
- **Typography:** One geometric sans for headings (e.g., Inter, General Sans, or Geist), one readable body sans. Generous line-height, large type scale for headlines (Apple/Linear scale — headlines should breathe).
- **Motion:** Subtle only — fade/slide on scroll reveal, smooth page transitions, no gratuitous 3D unless it serves a section (e.g., a light particle/aurora backdrop on hero only). Framer Motion + Lenis smooth scroll are enough; skip Three.js unless a specific moment calls for it.
- **Imagery:** Real screenshots (accounting app, company website, Travora) over illustrations. Certificates shown as clean scanned cards, not raw photos.
- **Tone of UI copy:** Direct, confident, understated. No exclamation marks. No "revolutionary."
