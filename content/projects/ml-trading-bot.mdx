sections:
  - id: hook
    heading: Reading the news instead of just the chart
    body: >-
      Most simple trading bots work purely off price data - moving averages, technical indicators. This one adds a different signal entirely - it reads financial news headlines, scores their sentiment with a finance-tuned language model, and trades on that.
    bullets:
      - Uses FinBERT, a BERT-based model fine-tuned specifically for financial sentiment, to score news as positive, negative, or neutral with a confidence value.
      - Built on LumiBot for the backtesting and trading engine, with the Alpaca API handling both the news feed and trade execution.
      - Supports paper trading for safe testing before anything touches real capital.
  - id: pipeline
    heading: From headline to trade
    body: >-
      The pipeline pulls news, scores it, and only acts on genuinely high-confidence signals - a design choice meant to avoid trading on noise.
    bullets:
      - Fetches the past three days of news for a given symbol via the Alpaca News API.
      - Runs headlines through FinBERT (PyTorch/Transformers) to get a sentiment label and a confidence score.
      - Only triggers a buy on positive sentiment above 99.9% confidence, and only exits on negative sentiment at the same threshold - neutral or lower-confidence signals just hold.
  - id: risk
    heading: Risk controls around every trade
    body: >-
      A sentiment signal alone isn't a trading strategy - the bot wraps every trade in automatic risk controls rather than relying on the signal being right every time.
    bullets:
      - Every buy is a bracket order with an automatic take-profit (+20%) and stop-loss (-5%) attached.
      - Position sizing is dynamic, based on account equity and a configurable cash-at-risk percentage rather than a fixed share count.
  - id: results
    heading: Backtest results
    body: >-
      Backtested against Yahoo Finance historical data over the 2025-2026 period, starting from $100,000 in simulated capital.
    bullets:
      - 15% total return over the backtest period, versus $100,000 initial to $115,000 final portfolio value.
      - 62% win rate across trades, with a maximum drawdown of -8% and a Sharpe ratio of 1.45.
      - Results are from backtesting, not live trading - sentiment-driven signals can behave very differently in live, volatile markets.
